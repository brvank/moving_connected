# Privacy Policy for Moving Connected

**Effective Date:** November 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 25, 2026  

---

### 1. Overview
**Moving Connected** ("the App") is an offline 2D multi-avatar navigation puzzle game developed by **Studio Arcabh** ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

> **Summary:** Moving Connected does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information or device data. The game operates completely offline.

---

### 2. Information Collection and Use
* **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or IP address).
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to play the game.
* **Local Game Data:** Game progress (e.g., unlocked levels and settings) is stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall or clear app data.

---

### 3. Device Permissions & Network Access
* **No Internet Access Required:** The App does not require or request internet access (`android.permission.INTERNET` is disabled). The game runs entirely offline.
* **No Sensitive Permissions:** The App does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, storage, or device identifiers.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services & Advertising
* **No Third-Party SDKs:** The App does not integrate any third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics or Firebase) or advertising networks (such as AdMob or Unity Ads).
* **No Tracking:** We do not track user activity across applications or websites, and we do not participate in cross-app data sharing.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)
Moving Connected is designed for general audiences and is safe for players of all ages, including children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because we do not collect any personal data from any user, we comply fully with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**.

---

### 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". Because the App does not collect contact information, we encourage you to review this policy periodically.
